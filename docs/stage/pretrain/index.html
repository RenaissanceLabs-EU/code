Stage 01

Data: pretrain and mid-train

Raw code, cleaned and mixed. Where a model learns what code looks like.

Who needs this

Most useful if you train from scratch or continue pretraining. If you only have a few GPUs, you will mostly use this stage to borrow a filtered subset or to understand what your base model already saw.

Decisions you will make

  • Continue pretraining a good open base model, or start from scratch. For almost everyone the first option wins on cost: a strong open code base model already carries most of the signal.
  • How much to filter. Quality filters (educational or textbook-style classifiers, license filters, deduplication) usually pay back more than adding raw volume.
  • Whether to add a short high-quality annealing or mid-training phase (synthetic, reasoning-heavy, repo-level data) at the end of pretraining.

Pitfalls

  • Near-duplicate files inflate benchmark scores and waste tokens. Deduplicate at file and repository level before you train.
  • Benchmark contamination: remove problems and solutions from your eval sets from the training mix, then confirm with a held-out or post-cutoff eval.
  • Licenses are per file. A corpus being open to download does not mean every file in it is permissively licensed. Read the card and keep the license metadata.

Stage 05

Evaluation

Benchmarks and harnesses. How you know whether anything worked.

Who needs this

Everyone. Run a small fixed set of evals before you change anything and after every stage. Prefer evals that refresh or are hard to contaminate, and keep one private set of your own.

Decisions you will make

  • A fast smoke test (minutes) and a slower gate (hours) so you can iterate without waiting on the expensive one.
  • At least one execution-based eval that matches your goal: function-level, repository-level, terminal, or SQL.
  • How to report: pass@1 with a fixed sampling setup, the number of samples, and the seed or a confidence interval.

Pitfalls

  • Saturated or contaminated benchmarks make every model look good. Track a time-sliced or private eval next to the classic ones.
  • Different harnesses give different scores for the same model. Pin the harness version and prompt format, and compare like with like.
  • Some benchmark instances have flawed tests or ambiguous statements. Read failures by hand before you trust a small difference.

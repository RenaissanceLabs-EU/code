Kimi-Dev: Agentless Training as Skill Prior

Argues Agentless-style training induces localization/edit/reflection priors; the abstract reports 60.4% on SWE-bench Verified (workflow mode) and 48.6% pass@1 as an agent after SFT on 5k public trajectories.

How to use it

Train single-turn verifiable steps first, then adapt to agents with small trajectory SFT.

Caveats

Numbers are authors' own.

The Stack v2 (dedup)

Near-deduplicated variant of The Stack v2 (metadata plus SWH blob ids) for building custom training mixes.

How to use it

Use when you want to apply your own quality and license filters on top of a deduplicated base.

Caveats

Same gating, SWH download and license caveats as The Stack v2. Dedup is MinHash-based and cross-file, not repo-aware.

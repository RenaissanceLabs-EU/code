CodeElo

Competition-level benchmark that submits solutions to Codeforces to obtain human-comparable Elo ratings, avoiding the need for private tests.

How to use it

Use for an Elo-style competition rating of reasoning models; heavy because it depends on live Codeforces submission.

Caveats

Submission-based (execution on Codeforces judge). Relies on an external platform and its rate limits; the paper motivates it by missing private tests and special judges in LiveCodeBench and USACO. Dataset and repo (QwenLM/CodeElo, no license field) were last updated in early 2025.

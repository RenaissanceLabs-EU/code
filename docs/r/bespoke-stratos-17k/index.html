Bespoke-Stratos-17k

DeepSeek-R1 distillation of the Sky-T1 recipe (code, math, science, puzzles) used for Bespoke-Stratos models; made at about $800 of generation per the card.

How to use it

Small proof that ~17K good traces move reasoning benchmarks; mostly useful as a reference recipe.

Caveats

Generated by DeepSeek-R1. Small and early; superseded by larger sets.
